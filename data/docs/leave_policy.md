# Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year,
accrued monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month. PTO requests must be
submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for
requests longer than 2 days. Unused PTO can be carried over into the next
year up to a maximum of 5 days; anything beyond that is forfeited on
December 31st unless local law requires otherwise. Sick leave is separate
from PTO and is not capped, but absences longer than 3 consecutive days
require a doctor's note submitted to HR.
