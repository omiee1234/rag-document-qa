# Leave Policy — UK

UK-based employees receive 25 days of annual leave plus 8 public bank
holidays, for a total of 33 days per year. Leave is accrued from the
first day of employment and must be booked at least 2 weeks in advance
through the HR portal. Unlike the US policy, UK leave has no company-
imposed carryover cap, though statutory rules may still apply. UK
employees are also entitled to Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) for illness
lasting more than 3 consecutive days, separate from annual leave.
