# Onboarding Policy — Contractors

Contractors and temporary staff complete an abbreviated 2-day onboarding
covering system access, confidentiality obligations, and safety
training, rather than the full 2-week employee program. Contractors are
provisioned only the system access required for their specific
engagement, reviewed and approved by the hiring manager. All contractors
must sign a confidentiality and IP assignment agreement before receiving
any system access. Contractors are not eligible for the standard 90-day
check-in process used for full-time hires.
