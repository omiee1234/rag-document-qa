# Termination Policy — Involuntary Layoff

Employees separated due to a layoff or reduction in force receive
severance pay calculated as 2 weeks of base pay per year of service,
with a minimum of 4 weeks, in addition to the final paycheck and any
accrued PTO payout. Laid-off employees retain access to company health
benefits under COBRA continuation, with the company covering the premium
for the first month. Unlike a voluntary resignation, laid-off employees
are not required to give advance notice and are offered outplacement
support through an external career services provider.
