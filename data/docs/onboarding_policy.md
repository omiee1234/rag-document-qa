# Onboarding Policy

New employees complete a 2-week onboarding program covering company
systems, compliance training, and role-specific shadowing. IT equipment
(laptop, monitor, badge) is provisioned before the employee's start date
based on information submitted by the hiring manager at least 10 business
days prior. All new hires must complete mandatory compliance training,
including data security and anti-harassment modules, within their first
30 days. A 90-day check-in with the hiring manager and HR is required to
confirm the new hire is meeting expectations.
