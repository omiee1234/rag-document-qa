# Termination and Offboarding Policy

Employees who voluntarily resign are asked to provide at least 2 weeks'
notice in writing to their manager and HR. On an employee's last day, IT
must revoke all system access and collect company equipment, including
laptops and access badges. Final paychecks, including any accrued and
unused PTO payout required by state law, are issued no later than the
next regular pay cycle. Employees separated involuntarily are offered an
exit interview and, where applicable, severance terms outlined in a
separate agreement.
