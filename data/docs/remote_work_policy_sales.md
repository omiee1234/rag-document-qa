# Remote Work Policy — Sales

Sales team members are expected to be in the office or on-site with
clients at least 3 days per week, since client-facing meetings are core
to the role. Fully remote arrangements are not available for sales roles
except in approved territory-based exceptions. Sales employees working
remotely must still be reachable during their assigned territory's
business hours. Travel to client sites is reimbursed separately under
the expense policy's travel provisions.
