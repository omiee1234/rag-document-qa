# Expense Policy — International Travel

International business travel expenses are reimbursed separately from
standard domestic expenses. Airfare in economy class is covered for
trips under 6 hours and premium economy for longer flights, with advance
manager approval required. A daily per diem of $85 covers meals and
incidentals while traveling internationally, replacing the standard $60
domestic meal limit. Visa fees and required travel vaccinations are
fully reimbursable with a receipt. International travel should be booked
through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days in advance where
possible.
