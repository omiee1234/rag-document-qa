# Parental Leave Policy — Adoption and Foster Placement

Employees adopting a child or receiving a foster placement are eligible
for the same 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave as biological
parents, under the general parental leave policy. For adoption, the
12-month window to take leave begins on the date of legal placement
rather than a birth date. Employees are encouraged, but not required, to
notify HR as soon as an adoption or foster placement is confirmed, even
before the standard 30-day notice window, so HR can begin benefits
coordination early.
