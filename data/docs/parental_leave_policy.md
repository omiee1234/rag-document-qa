# Parental Leave Policy

Eligible employees receive 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave following
the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child, available to both
primary and secondary caregivers. Leave must be taken within 12 months of
the qualifying event and can be split into two blocks with manager
approval. Employees on parental leave continue to accrue PTO and remain
eligible for company health benefits during their leave. Employees should
notify HR at least 30 days before the expected start of leave when
possible.
