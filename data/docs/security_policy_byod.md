# Security Policy — Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Employees who wish to access company email or documents from a personal
phone or tablet must enroll the device in the company's mobile device
management (MDM) system. A BYOD device is subject to a remote wipe of
company data only, never personal data, if the device is lost or the
employee leaves the company. Personal devices used for BYOD must have a
passcode or biometric lock enabled at all times. BYOD access does not
extend to systems containing customer data, which require a company-
issued and encrypted laptop as described in the main security policy.
