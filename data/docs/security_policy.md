# Information Security Policy

All company laptops must have full-disk encryption and an approved
endpoint security agent installed before being used to access company
systems. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and multi-factor
authentication is required for all systems containing customer data.
Suspected security incidents, including lost devices, phishing emails, or
unauthorized access, must be reported to the security team within 24
hours. USB storage devices are prohibited on company laptops unless
explicitly approved by IT security.
