# Code of Conduct — Social Media

Employees posting on personal social media accounts must not share
confidential company information, unreleased product details, or
internal financial data. When discussing the company publicly, employees
should make clear that opinions expressed are their own and not the
company's official position. Employees representing the company on
official social media accounts must follow the brand and communications
team's guidelines. Violations involving confidential information are
treated as a breach of the code of conduct and may result in
disciplinary action.
