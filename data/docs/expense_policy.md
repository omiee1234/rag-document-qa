# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Business expenses under $75 can be submitted with a receipt through the
expense portal and are typically reimbursed within 7 business days.
Expenses over $500 require prior written approval from a manager before
they are incurred. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $60
per day; alcohol is not reimbursable except at client-facing dinners
capped at $40 per person. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the
current IRS standard mileage rate. All expense reports must be submitted
within 60 days of the expense being incurred, or they will not be
reimbursed.
