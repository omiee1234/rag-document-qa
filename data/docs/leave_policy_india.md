# Leave Policy — India

Employees based in India receive 18 days of earned leave and 12 days of
casual leave per calendar year, along with 10 public holidays per the
regional holiday calendar. Casual leave cannot be carried over and lapses
at year end, while earned leave can be carried over up to 15 days.
Leave requests must be approved by the reporting manager through the HR
portal at least 3 working days in advance. Maternity leave in India
follows separate statutory guidelines administered by HR.
