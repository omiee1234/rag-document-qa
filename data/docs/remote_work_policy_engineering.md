# Remote Work Policy — Engineering

Engineering employees may work fully remote with team lead approval,
given the team's asynchronous collaboration practices. Engineers are
expected to overlap with their team's core collaboration window of at
least 4 hours per day and attend the weekly in-person or video sync.
Equipment shipped to remote engineers includes a company laptop and the
same $500 home office stipend as the general remote work policy.
Engineers relocating internationally must still notify HR at least 30
days in advance for tax compliance.
